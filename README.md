# ⚔ CLIP-FORGE

## Forge Your Esports Legacy

Clip-Forge is an esports talent discovery and team-building platform designed to connect players, teams, and esports organisations.

Our mission is to create a competitive ecosystem where gamers can build their identity, discover opportunities, and grow within esports.

---

# 🎮 Platform Vision

Clip-Forge aims to provide:

- Player esports profiles
- Team recruitment systems
- Squad discovery
- Competitive gaming networks
- Tournament ecosystems
- Highlight and achievement showcases

---

# 🏆 The Problem

Many talented players struggle to find:

- Competitive teams
- Like-minded teammates
- Esports opportunities
- Recognition for their skills

Clip-Forge is designed to bridge that gap.
